How hard should you train?

Use 2–3 RIR on most compounds. On safer isolation or machine work, the last set can occasionally reach 0–1 RIR after your technique is consistent.

RIR = “reps in reserve.” If you could do 2 more clean reps, that set was about 2 RIR. Stop when form starts breaking.

Progression rule

Keep the same weight until you reach the top of the prescribed rep range on all working sets with good form. Then increase by the smallest practical increment.
